# For probate attorneys and personal representatives

When an estate includes a house that's hard to sell, ARCH Relief can be a straightforward buyer. We buy Twin Cities properties that a traditional listing struggles with: estates with title defects, delinquent taxes, deferred maintenance, or heirs who don't agree.

## What we buy

- Houses held in open or stalled estates, sold by a personal representative.
- Properties where probate was never opened and heirs are working toward a decree of descent.
- Properties with delinquent taxes, old liens, unreleased mortgages or breaks in the chain of title.
- Individual heirs' undivided interests, when not every heir is ready to sell.
- Houses that need work. The estate doesn't need to clean out or repair the property first.

## How we work with an estate

1. **A short call.** Tell us about the property, the estate's status and the timeline. We'll tell you plainly whether we're a fit.
2. **A written offer to the personal representative or heirs.** Terms in writing, with nothing to sign until you and your client have reviewed it.
3. **Your process, your timing.** We work around court approval, notice periods and any spousal consent the sale requires. Your client's counsel stays in charge of the estate.
4. **Closing through a licensed title company.** Taxes, liens and costs are paid from the sale, and title work is coordinated with the title company.

## Why attorneys work with us

- **We do the title homework.** We research the deed history, tax status and heirs before making an offer, and share what we find.
- **No surprise contingencies.** Our offers say what they're conditioned on.
- **We're local and reachable.** A Minnesota company with a real team, meeting by appointment at Carlson Towers in Minnetonka.
